# TweakiTok <img src="https://img.icons8.com/color/48/tiktok--v1.png" width="28" height="28" valign="middle" /> 47.5.3

An awesome, modern TikTok tweak for iOS.

## Features

- **No Ads**: Removes in-feed and sponsored ads.
- **Live Stream Recording**: 60fps internal audio/video recording with controls and gallery export.
- **Circular 3-Dot Action Menu**: Quick-access menu above avatar for downloads, links, and clean screen.
- **Watermark-Free Video Downloads**: Save videos directly to Camera Roll without watermarks.
- **Video Cover Image Download**: Save high-res video cover images from the download sheet.
- **HD Video Downloads**: Option to download videos in high definition.
- **Story Downloads**: Download photos and videos from stories directly.
- **Story Progress Bar & Seekbar**: Interactive draggable progress bar on stories.
- **Photos & Albums Download**: Download individual photos or batch-download full albums.
- **Download Current Photo**: One-tap download for the active photo in multi-slide posts.
- **Audio / Music Download**: Save audio tracks and background music directly.
- **Clean Screen Mode**: Hide all UI overlays, buttons, captions, and comments.
- **Smart Auto-Hide**: Automatically hides tweak buttons during 2x fast-forward long press.
- **Language Support (EN & TR)**: Auto-adapts to app language or toggleable anytime in settings.
- **Follow Status Badge**: Profile badge indicating whether a user follows you back.
- **Copy Actions**: One-tap copy for video descriptions, video URLs, and audio links.
- **Auto-Play**: Automatically advances to the next video when playback finishes.
- **Seekbar & Progress Bar**: Persistent draggable seekbar on all videos.
- **Action Confirmations**: Prompts before liking, disliking, or following.
- **Profile Enhancements**: Save HD avatars, copy bio details, and view total video counts.
- **Always Show Publish Date**: Displays video publish date next to username on all feeds.
- **Copy Comment Without Username**: Copies comment text without the author's handle.
- **Remember Clear Display**: Keeps clear display mode active when scrolling across videos.
- **Resume Videos After Scrolling**: Resumes playback from where you left off when returning to a previous video.
- **Keep Screen Refresh Rate (120 Hz)**: Prevents display refresh rate drops in videos, stories, and feed on ProMotion displays.
- **Video Metadata**: Shows upload date and like count on profile grids.
- **HD Uploads**: Forces video uploads in high definition.
- **Extended Text Limits**: Removes character limits for bio and comments.
- **External Browser**: Open links directly in Safari.
- **Region Changer**: Built-in region selector to browse TikTok feeds from any country.
- **App Lock**: Biometric Face ID / Touch ID and passcode protection.
- **Privacy & Ghost Mode**: Read DMs without seen receipts and type without typing indicators.
- **Sanitize Sharing Links**: Strips tracking parameters (`_t`, `_r`, UTM, referrer) from shared links.
- **Disable Screen Capture Detection**: Blocks screenshot & recording detection and suppresses share popups.
- **Disable Telemetry**: Blocks background analytics and diagnostic tracking to ByteDance servers.
- **Custom Playback Speed**: Default playback speed selection (0.5x, 1.0x, 1.5x, 2.0x).
- **Offline Videos Limit**: Custom cache and offline video limits.
- **Hide Live Streams**: Option to skip and hide live streams from the feed.
- **Skip Recommendations**: Auto-skips recommended user cards and suggestion banners.
- **Disable Pull to Refresh**: Prevents accidental feed refresh gestures.
- **Disable Sensitive Content Warnings**: Bypasses intrusive content warning screens.
- **Restart Notice Banner**: Adaptive toast notification prompting restart when settings change.
- **Security Bypasses**: Bypasses jailbreak detection and environment checks.
